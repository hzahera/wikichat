# Your branding

Put your organization's logo here, for example `org-logo.svg` or `org-logo.png`, and point to it in `.env`:

```ini
WIKICHAT_ORG_LOGO_FILE=branding/org-logo.svg
```

It shows at the bottom of the sidebar and links to `WIKICHAT_ORG_URL`.
A wide logo about 120 x 24 pixels (or an SVG) looks best.
